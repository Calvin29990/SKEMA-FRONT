# Report text — approved CIB figures

## Figure 1. European CIB footprint and platform architecture

This figure introduces the geographic and business architecture of the comparison. It combines selected publicly documented hubs for BNP Paribas CIB and CACIB with the main activity engines shown in public business-line materials.

- Dots and city labels represent selected public hub signals, not an exhaustive legal-entity footprint.
- The right-hand architecture separates financing, markets and securities services; it is a capability map rather than a revenue chart.
- Links and highlighted nodes are directional reading aids only: they do not represent transaction volumes, revenue or booking location.

**Key figures — three lines**

- **7 selected hubs:** Paris, London, Frankfurt, Milan, Madrid, Stockholm and Luxembourg.
- **3 BNP Paribas CIB engines:** Global Banking, Global Markets and Securities Services.
- **3 CACIB engines:** Financing Activities, Market Activities and Investment Banking.

**Executive takeaway.** The European platform is connected, but Paris and Luxembourg must remain separate in the next comparison because the public sources do not evidence identical local perimeters.

**Sources to verify.** [BNP Paribas Securities Services — Our locations](https://securities.cib.bnpparibas/who-we-are/our-locations/); [CACIB — Leveraged Finance Europe locations](https://www.ca-cib.com/en/expertise/solutions-support-your-financing-strategy/supporting-your-financing-needs/leveraged-0); [CACIB Finance Luxembourg prospectus](https://www.ca-cib.com/sites/default/files/2024-03/Prospectus_CACIB%20USD70,000,000%20Callable_Zero_Coupon_Notes_due_January_2062.pdf); [BNP Paribas CIB — At a glance](https://cib.bnpparibas/cib-at-a-glance/); [CACIB 2025 activity report](https://activity-report.ca-cib.com/key-figures-and-awards/).

## Figure 2. Four franchises: client reach, offer breadth and operating weight

This executive bubble chart focuses the comparison on four anchors: BNP CIB France, CACIB France, CACEIS Luxembourg and BNP CIB Luxembourg.

- Left to right means a narrower or broader publicly named offer; this is a qualitative positioning axis, not a performance score.
- The vertical bands distinguish a local signal, public local reach and a group platform; the large labels retain the public client signal where it exists.
- Bubble size uses public staff signals as a visual proxy for operating weight; N/D means that the local client count is not publicly disclosed. Neither is revenue, assets, market share or client value.

**Key figures — three lines**

- **BNP CIB France:** 19k+ corporate and institutional clients; 42k staff.
- **CACIB France:** approximately 3.6k clients; 10.8k staff.
- **Luxembourg signals:** CACEIS 240+ clients* and 850 staff; BNP CIB Luxembourg N/D clients and 72 staff.

**Executive takeaway.** BNP CIB and CACIB read as broad group platforms; CACEIS reads as specialised asset servicing; BNP CIB Luxembourg reads as a focused local CIB offer.

**Sources to verify.** [BNP Paribas CIB — At a glance](https://cib.bnpparibas/cib-at-a-glance/); [BNP Paribas — 2026 At a Glance](https://cdn-group.bnpparibas.com/uploads/file/BNPParibas_2026_At_A_Glance_EN.pdf); [CACIB — 2025 key figures](https://activity-report.ca-cib.com/key-figures-and-awards/); [CACIB — Discover Crédit Agricole CIB](https://www.ca-cib.com/en/about-us/discover-credit-agricole-cib); [BNP Paribas Luxembourg — Corporate and Institutional Banking](https://www.bnpparibas.lu/en/businesses/corporate-and-institutional-client/corporate-institutional-banking/); [CACEIS Luxembourg Investor Services report](https://www.caceis.com/fileadmin/documents/pdf/Who-We-Are/Where-to-find-us/Luxembourg/2023-CACEIS-IS-Bank-CSR-Report.pdf); [CACEIS 2025 brochure](https://www.caceis.com/fileadmin/documents/pdf/Insights/Corporate/2025/BROCHURE_2025_EN.pdf).

\* CACEIS's reported 240+ client perimeter extends beyond Luxembourg to Continental Europe and offshore platforms. BNP CIB Luxembourg's local client count is not publicly disclosed.

## Scope note

The two figures are explanatory visuals, not a four-way revenue ranking. BNP CIB and CACIB group signals are not France-only; CACEIS client scope extends beyond Luxembourg in the cited report; BNP CIB Luxembourg client count is not publicly disclosed. The client and staff figures are public signals with different perimeters and should not be read as strictly comparable local-market measures.
