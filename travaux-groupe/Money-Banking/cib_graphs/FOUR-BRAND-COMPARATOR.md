# France–Luxembourg four-brand comparator

`output/four_brand_comparator/FOUR_BRANDS_ACTIVITY_COMPARATOR.png` is the product/client version of the comparator. The previous Europe map already explains the locations; this visual is deliberately focused on **what each platform delivers and to which client universe**.

It is limited to four visible brand anchors:

- BNP Paribas CIB — France / group-level signal;
- Crédit Agricole CIB — France / group-level signal;
- BNP Paribas CIB — Luxembourg / local entity signal;
- CACEIS Investor Services — Luxembourg / local entity signal.

## What the visual adds

- A top card for each platform with its public scale signal and client coverage.
- A product matrix covering capital markets/risk, financing/advisory, securities services, asset/fund services and specialist local offers.
- A client-segment band identifying the public client universes: corporates, financial institutions, institutional investors, public sector, asset managers, funds, banks/brokers and private capital.
- A visible distinction between a group-level platform and a local legal-entity disclosure.

Filled cells mean that the activity or service is publicly named for the selected platform. They are not revenue shares, market-share scores or a complete product catalogue.

## Why there is no single camembert

A single pie chart would require one common denominator for BNP CIB, CACIB, BNP CIB Luxembourg and CACEIS Luxembourg. Public sources do not provide that common perimeter. The visual therefore uses a product/client architecture and keeps the public scale signals in their original scopes instead of creating a false four-way ranking.
