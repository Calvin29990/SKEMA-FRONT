# France–Luxembourg four-brand comparator

`output/four_brand_comparator/FOUR_BRANDS_ACTIVITY_COMPARATOR.png` is a new alternative to the rejected client-case visuals. It is deliberately limited to four visible brand anchors:

- BNP Paribas CIB — France / group-level signal;
- Crédit Agricole CIB — France / group-level signal;
- BNP Paribas CIB — Luxembourg / local entity signal;
- CACEIS Investor Services — Luxembourg / local entity signal.

## Why the chart is split into two evidence layers

A single bubble scale would be misleading: BNP CIB and CACIB publish group-level figures, while the Luxembourg sources describe different local legal entities and business models. The visual therefore uses:

1. **Group platform scale** — BNP CIB and CACIB 2025 published NBI signals.
2. **Luxembourg local footprint** — public staff/activity or staff/client signals, explicitly labelled as local evidence and not a league table.
3. **France–Luxembourg map** — location and brand architecture, not a booking-location or revenue map.

The chart keeps the four logos visible but does not claim that the four perimeters are directly comparable. `data/four_brand_public_signals.csv` contains the exact signal, date, scope and source for every number.
