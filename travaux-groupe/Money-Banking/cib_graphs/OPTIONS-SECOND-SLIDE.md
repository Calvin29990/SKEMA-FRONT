# Three options for slide 2 — after the Europe map

Audience: former M1 students, finance committee mindset, five-minute pitch. Each visual is designed to be read in under 20 seconds and explained in less than one minute.

## Option 1 — Where the quarterly momentum comes from

**Graph:** horizontal dot plot with a zero line; BNP Paribas CIB and CACIB / Large Customers are grouped in the same frame. Totals are bold; business engines are lighter.

**Question answered:** Which business engines explain the latest quarter?

**Why it works:** It is the most finance-committee-friendly option: direct, quantitative and based on the latest official Q2 2026 results. It follows the map with a performance answer rather than another architecture diagram.

**Oral takeaway:** BNP’s Q2 momentum is led by Global Markets and Securities Services; CACIB shows positive but more moderate momentum, led by Capital Markets & Investment Banking.

**Caveat:** CACIB uses Crédit Agricole S.A. Large Customers / CIB-related labels, not an identical BNP CIB reporting perimeter.

## Option 2 — From Q1 headwinds to Q2 acceleration

**Graph:** two small-multiple double curves, one panel for BNP and one for CACIB. The x-axis is Q1 2026 to Q2 2026; the y-axis is year-on-year revenue change.

**Question answered:** Did the direction of performance change during 2026?

**Why it works:** The audience immediately sees the inflection. BNP CIB moves from -0.8% to +12.7%; CACIB Corporate & Investment Banking moves from -4.0% to +4.4%. The business-line curves show what drove the change.

**Oral takeaway:** The second quarter materially changed the short-term narrative: markets and securities services accelerated at BNP, while CACIB moved back into positive territory with capital markets leading.

**Caveat:** Q1 and Q2 are reported against their respective prior-year quarters; the two banks remain in separate panels.

## Option 3 — From European hubs to client solutions

**Graph:** a graphical operating-model flow with rounded nodes and curved arrows. It is not an ASCII diagram and it does not use table cells. Equal-width links are intentional.

**Question answered:** What does the European network actually deliver to clients?

**Why it works:** It is the most intuitive bridge between the map and the rest of the pitch. The map explains the geography; this slide explains the CIB value chain.

**Oral takeaway:** The same European network supports different activity architectures: BNP combines Global Banking, Global Markets and Securities Services; CACIB combines Financing, Market Activities and Investment Banking.

**Caveat:** The flow is strategic, not revenue-weighted. No stream thickness is used to invent market share or revenue volumes.

## Recommendation

- Choose **Option 1** for the strongest finance-committee slide.
- Choose **Option 2** if the pitch needs a visible performance inflection.
- Choose **Option 3** if the group needs a simple business-model explanation immediately after the map.

The three candidate images are in `output/options/`. Do not integrate one into the final six-slide deck until the group chooses.
