# Four-brand executive bubble chart

`output/four_brand_comparator/FOUR_BRANDS_EXECUTIVE_BUBBLE.png` follows the requested executive bubble-chart format.

- **Vertical reading:** three simple bands — local signal, public local reach and group platform. This avoids a misleading log scale for an M1 audience; the actual public client signal is written directly next to each bubble.
- **Horizontal reading:** qualitative breadth of the publicly named offer — specialist, mixed or broad offer. This is a positioning axis, not a performance score.
- **Bubble area:** public employee signal, used as a visual indication of operating weight; it is not revenue, assets, market share or client value.
- **Right-hand read-outs:** the product families and client perimeter behind each bubble.

## Four anchors

- **BNP CIB FR:** 19k+ corporate and institutional clients; 42k staff; broad capital markets, financing, securities services, risk, cash and financial advice offer.
- **CACIB FR:** approximately 3.6k clients; 10.8k staff; capital markets, financing, investment banking and sustainable/trade finance offer.
- **CACEIS Luxembourg:** 240+ reported clients and 850 local staff; custody, fund administration, depositary, shareholder services, treasury/markets and private capital. The cited client perimeter includes Luxembourg, Continental Europe and offshore platforms.
- **BNP CIB Luxembourg:** 72 staff and a specialist five-function local CIB offer; the local client count is not publicly disclosed and is intentionally shown as `N/D`, not estimated.
