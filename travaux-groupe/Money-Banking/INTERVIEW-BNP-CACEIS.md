## 2. Ce qu'il faut savoir pour les deux entretiens

### Pitch de 60 secondes

Structure :

1. MSc Corporate Financial Management et intérêt pour la banque ;
2. expérience ou preuves concrètes : finance, Excel, CIB, marchés, analyse ;
3. pourquoi le métier ciblé ;
4. pourquoi cette institution et ce pays ;
5. ce que tu peux apporter comme stagiaire/analyste.

Ne pas réciter le travail de groupe. Utiliser le travail comme preuve que tu comprends le secteur.

### Questions communes

- Tell me about yourself.
- Why banking and why now?
- Why France / Luxembourg?
- Why CIB rather than retail banking, asset management or consulting?
- What have you learned recently about our business?
- Describe a time you analysed a difficult problem.
- Describe a time you worked in a group with unclear responsibilities.
- Tell me about a mistake and what you changed afterwards.
- What financial market topic are you following?
- What questions do you have for us?

## 3. CACEIS Luxembourg — préparation spécifique

### Modèle économique à comprendre

CACEIS est une banque d'asset servicing dédiée principalement aux asset managers et aux investisseurs institutionnels. La chaîne de valeur à connaître comprend :

- execution et clearing ;
- foreign exchange ;
- securities lending ;
- custody ;
- depositary ;
- fund administration ;
- fund distribution support ;
- middle-office outsourcing ;
- issuer services.

### Questions techniques probables

- What is the difference between custody and depositary services?
- What is fund administration?
- Why do asset managers outsource middle-office and back-office activities?
- What are settlement, reconciliation and corporate actions?
- What is securities lending and what are its risks?
- How does a fund administrator reduce operational risk?
- Why is Luxembourg important for investment funds?
- How do regulation, data quality and automation affect asset servicing?
- What is the difference between a fund manager's investment decision and an asset servicer's operational role?

### Sujet d'actualité à préparer

Le communiqué du 6 octobre 2026 annonce que CACEIS va acquérir l'activité d'asset servicing pour compte de tiers d'Edmond de Rothschild au Luxembourg, sous réserve de l'approbation de la CSSF. Le point intéressant pour l'entretien : CACEIS ne se contente pas de conserver des titres ; il devient une infrastructure stratégique pour les fonds, avec des enjeux de scale, de technologie, de sécurité opérationnelle et de consolidation du marché.

Questions à poser :

- How will this transaction change the operating model in Luxembourg?
- Which activities are most affected: custody, fund administration or depositary?
- What technology investments matter most for the integration?
- How does CACEIS differentiate itself from other European asset servicers?

## 4. BNP Paribas CIB — préparation spécifique

### Trois blocs à maîtriser

1. **Global Banking** — loans, transaction banking, capital raising, advisory and structured finance.
2. **Global Markets** — rates, FX, credit, equities, derivatives, sales and trading, including FICC and Equity & Prime Services.
3. **Securities Services** — custody, clearing, settlement and asset servicing.

### Sujet d'actualité à préparer

Dans les résultats du deuxième trimestre 2026, BNP Paribas indique que CIB a progressé de 12,7 % sur un an, avec un trimestre particulièrement fort pour Global Markets et Securities Services. Global Banking reste présenté comme un moteur de leadership en banque d'investissement en EMEA. Il faut savoir expliquer les moteurs possibles sans transformer une variation trimestrielle en tendance permanente.

Questions techniques probables :

- What is the difference between Global Banking and Global Markets?
- What do FICC and Equity & Prime Services cover?
- How does a bank make money from a corporate financing transaction?
- What is the role of a sales person compared with a trader?
- How does a bank hedge an FX or interest-rate exposure?
- What are counterparty risk, market risk and liquidity risk?
- Why are capital markets important for European corporates?
- How can CIB benefit from a universal-bank model?
- How do volatility and interest rates affect Global Markets revenues?

### Questions à poser à BNP

- How are Global Banking, Global Markets and Securities Services coordinated for the same client?
- Which skills distinguish a strong junior in the team?
- How much of the business is relationship-driven versus electronic?
- How is the team adapting to AI and automation while preserving control and client trust?
- What is the balance between technical knowledge, commercial judgement and execution skills?

## 5. Comparaison pour le devoir et pour l'entretien

| Question | BNP Paribas CIB | CACEIS Luxembourg |
|---|---|---|
| Client principal | Corporates, financial institutions, institutional investors | Asset managers, funds, institutional investors |
| Fonction | Finance, markets, advisory, hedging | Custody, administration, depositary and post-trade services |
| Revenus | Interest income, fees, spreads, underwriting and trading-related revenues | Service and administration fees |
| Risques | Credit, market, counterparty, liquidity and regulatory risks | Operational, custody, settlement, cyber and regulatory risks |
| Angle entretien | Client franchise, capital markets and risk solutions | Reliability, controls, data, process and technology |

### Formule de conclusion

> BNP Paribas CIB helps clients raise capital, access markets and manage financial risk, while CACEIS provides the post-trade and asset-servicing infrastructure that allows funds and institutional investors to operate securely. They are not identical competitors; they are complementary parts of institutional finance.

## 7. Règles de prudence

- Les faits d'entretien doivent venir de sources publiques.
- Ne pas révéler le nom d'un interlocuteur ou une information reçue pendant un processus de recrutement.
- Ne pas dire qu'une banque est « intéressée » sans confirmation officielle.
- Ne pas comparer les actifs d'un groupe BNP avec ceux d'une entité locale CACEIS sans harmoniser le périmètre, la date et la devise.
