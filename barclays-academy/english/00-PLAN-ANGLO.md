## La rotation HireVue — 14 questions (1/jour, cycle de 2 semaines)
1. Walk me through your CV (2 ans pour SKEMA = l'histoire de discipline)
2. Why Barclays? / Why this programme?
3. Why London? Why the UK?
4. A time you worked under pressure and delivered
5. A mistake you made and what you learned *(→ utilise le rapport DB : « feedback revealed Analysis and Curiosity as growth areas; here's what I changed »)*
6. A time you worked in a team with a difficult dynamic
7. A time you paid attention to detail (ta force DB : *Detail*)
8. Something in the markets that caught your attention this week *(teste le rituel Bloomberg)*
9. Explain a financial concept to a client who is not technical
10. A time you took responsibility for an outcome (ta force DB : *Personal Responsibility*)
11. Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly *(ta force : Collaboration + discipline)*
12. What does Integrity / Service / Excellence / Stewardship mean to you in practice? (RISES)
13. Do you require visa sponsorship? *(réponse 20 s prête : « Yes — I'd need Skilled Worker sponsorship; I meet the new-entrant criteria and Barclays states it provides sponsorship for successful applicants »)*
14. What questions do you have for us? (en préparer 3, jamais 0)
