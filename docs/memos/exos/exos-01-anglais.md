## A. Aisance / phrases — complète le trou

*Piège général : les leurres sont des prépositions/français littéral. Ne traduis pas : retrouve l'expression figée.*

**1.** The new regulation will come ___ force on 1 January.
A) on · B) in · C) into · D) at
→ **C) into** — *Piège :* « in force » n'existe pas : c'est **come into force** (entrer en vigueur).

**2.** Payment must be made ___ the 15th at the latest.
A) on · B) at · C) until · D) by
→ **D) by** — *Piège :* **by** = au plus tard (date limite) ; « until the 15th » = jusqu'au 15 (durée).

**3.** We will deliver the goods ___ time.
A) in · B) on · C) at · D) by
→ **B) on** — *Piège :* **on time** = à l'heure ; **in time** = à temps (avant la limite).

**4.** She signed the contract ___ behalf of the company.
A) for · B) at · C) on · D) in
→ **C) on** — *Piège :* expression figée : **on behalf of** (au nom de).

**5.** The report focuses ___ cost reduction.
A) at · B) on · C) in · D) over
→ **B) on** — *Piège :* **focus on** ; « focus in » = faute fréquente.

**6.** They invested heavily ___ renewable energy.
A) at · B) to · C) in · D) on
→ **C) in** — *Piège :* **invest in** (pas « invest on »).

**7.** The merger was completed ___ the end of March.
A) on · B) at · C) in · D) by
→ **D) by** — *Piège :* **by the end of** = avant la fin de ; « at the end of » = au moment de la fin.

**8.** Profits rose ___ 12% last year.
A) at · B) by · C) to · D) of
→ **B) by** — *Piège :* montant de la hausse = **by** ; niveau atteint = **to**.

**9.** Sales increased ___ 12% to reach €4m.
A) of · B) at · C) by · D) for
→ **C) by** — *Piège :* idem : la progression se dit **by**, jamais « of ».

**10.** He is in charge ___ the audit team.
A) for · B) about · C) with · D) of
→ **D) of** — *Piège :* **in charge of** ; « responsible for » mais « in charge of ».

**11.** The project consists ___ three phases.
A) from · B) of · C) in · D) with
→ **B) of** — *Piège :* **consist of** ; « consist in » = consister à (sens différent).

**12.** We are looking forward ___ your reply.
A) on · B) for · C) at · D) to
→ **D) to** — *Piège :* **look forward to + nom/-ing** (jamais « look forward to hear »).

**13.** The board agreed ___ the proposal.
A) for · B) on · C) to · D) with
→ **C) to** — *Piège :* **agree to** une proposition / **agree with** une personne.

**14.** Please find ___ the annual report.
A) attached with · B) attached to · C) enclosed · D) enclosed in
→ **C) enclosed** — *Piège :* **enclosed / attached** seuls ; « attached with » = faute.

**15.** The subsidiary ___ by the CFO is based in Lyon.
A) ran · B) runs · C) running · D) run
→ **D) run** — *Piège :* participe passé passif : **run by** (dirigée par).

**16.** The loan was ___ by the credit committee.
A) approved · B) approve · C) approving · D) approval
→ **A) approved** — *Piège :* passif : was + participe passé (**approved**), pas le nom.

**17.** Interest rates have ___ significantly this year.
A) risen · B) rose · C) raised · D) rised
→ **A) risen** — *Piège :* **rise–rose–risen** (intransitif) ; « raise » = augmenter qqch.

**18.** The central bank ___ interest rates twice.
A) risen · B) raised · C) rose · D) rised
→ **B) raised** — *Piège :* **raise** = augmenter qqch (transitif) ; la banque « raises » les taux.

**19.** Our costs are ___ than last year.
A) high · B) higher · C) highest · D) more high
→ **B) higher** — *Piège :* comparatif de « high » = **higher** (jamais « more high »).

**20.** This was our ___ profitable quarter.
A) much · B) more · C) best · D) most
→ **D) most** — *Piège :* superlatif d'un adjectif long : **the most profitable**.

**21.** He works ___ than anyone in the team.
A) harder · B) hardest · C) more hard · D) hardly
→ **A) harder** — *Piège :* **harder** (comparatif de hard) ; « hardly » = à peine (piège de sens).

**22.** The invoice must be paid ___ 30 days.
A) during · B) within · C) to · D) for
→ **B) within** — *Piège :* **within 30 days** (dans un délai de) ; « during » = pendant un événement.

**23.** She has worked here ___ 2019.
A) from · B) for · C) during · D) since
→ **D) since** — *Piège :* **since** + point de départ ; **for** + durée.

**24.** We have known them ___ ten years.
A) during · B) for · C) from · D) since
→ **B) for** — *Piège :* **for** + durée (ten years).

**25.** The agreement was ___ yesterday.
A) signing · B) signed · C) sign · D) signs
→ **B) signed** — *Piège :* passif au passé : **was signed**.

**26.** He is ___ for the departmental budget.
A) responsive · B) responsibility · C) responsibly · D) responsible
→ **D) responsible** — *Piège :* adjectif après « be » : **responsible** (pas le nom ni l'adverbe).

**27.** The decision had a major ___ on our results.
A) impact · B) impacted · C) impacts · D) impacting
→ **A) impact** — *Piège :* après « a major » il faut un **nom** : impact.

**28.** We need to ___ our operating costs.
A) reduce · B) reduction · C) reducing · D) reductive
→ **A) reduce** — *Piège :* après « to » (infinitif de but) : **reduce**.

**29.** The ___ of the company rose sharply.
A) valued · B) valuable · C) valuably · D) value
→ **D) value** — *Piège :* après « the » : nom **value**.

**30.** Their offer was ___ generous.
A) extreme · B) extremely · C) extremity · D) extremes
→ **B) extremely** — *Piège :* adverbe devant un adjectif : **extremely generous**.

**31.** The audit was carried ___ thoroughly.
A) up · B) in · C) off · D) out
→ **D) out** — *Piège :* **carry out** = mener ; « carry on » = continuer.

**32.** The deal ___ through despite the delays.
A) went · B) ran · C) came · D) took
→ **A) went** — *Piège :* **go through** = aboutir ; « come through » = survivre/recevoir.

**33.** They had to ___ the meeting off.
A) take · B) put · C) call · D) make
→ **C) call** — *Piège :* **call off** = annuler.

**34.** Please ___ up the figures before Friday.
A) total · B) sum · C) add · D) count
→ **C) add** — *Piège :* **add up** = additionner (« total up » existe mais est rare ; « count up » ≠).

**35.** The company will ___ off 200 employees.
A) put · B) set · C) lay · D) take
→ **C) lay** — *Piège :* **lay off** = licencier ; « put off » = reporter.

**36.** We must ___ up with the new rules.
A) hold · B) catch · C) keep · D) stay
→ **C) keep** — *Piège :* **keep up with** = suivre le rythme ; « catch up with » = rattraper un retard.

**37.** She will ___ over as CFO in June.
A) take · B) make · C) get · D) put
→ **A) take** — *Piège :* **take over** = prendre la relève.

**38.** The old IT system was gradually ___ out.
A) put · B) taken · C) given · D) phased
→ **D) phased** — *Piège :* **phase out** = supprimer progressivement.

**39.** He ___ for the accounting error.
A) apologised · B) excused · C) forgave · D) regretted
→ **A) apologised** — *Piège :* **apologise for** = s'excuser de ; « excuse » = excuser qqn.

**40.** Prices are ___ to rise next quarter.
A) expected · B) expectedly · C) expects · D) expecting
→ **A) expected** — *Piège :* tournure passive : **be expected to**.

**41.** If the rate ___ , we will renegotiate.
A) rises · B) rised · C) raises · D) rose
→ **A) rises** — *Piège :* conditionnel réel : if + présent ; « rise » intransitif (le taux monte seul).

**42.** Unless payment ___ , supply will be suspended.
A) received · B) will receive · C) is received · D) receives
→ **C) is received** — *Piège :* passif + pas de « will » après unless : **is received**.

**43.** Yesterday the board ___ the annual results.
A) approve · B) approving · C) approved · D) approves
→ **C) approved** — *Piège :* **yesterday** → prétérit simple (pas de present perfect).

**44.** Tomorrow we ___ the new compliance policy.
A) have adopted · B) adopting · C) will adopt · D) adopted
→ **C) will adopt** — *Piège :* **tomorrow** → futur ; « adopt » = adopter, « adapt » = adapter.

**45.** The figures ___ been verified by the auditors.
A) have · B) is · C) are · D) has
→ **A) have** — *Piège :* **figures** = pluriel → have.

**46.** Neither the CFO nor the auditors ___ present.
A) is · B) was · C) been · D) were
→ **D) were** — *Piège :* accord avec le sujet le plus proche (auditors → pluriel).

**47.** A number of clients ___ complained about the fees.
A) have · B) has · C) was · D) is
→ **A) have** — *Piège :* **a number of** = pluriel (≠ « the number of » = singulier).

**48.** He suggested ___ the deadline.
A) extended · B) extending · C) extend · D) to extend
→ **B) extending** — *Piège :* **suggest + -ing** (jamais « suggest to do »).

**49.** We decided ___ the offer.
A) to accept · B) accepting · C) accept · D) accepted
→ **A) to accept** — *Piège :* **decide to + infinitif**.

**50.** They avoid ___ about the risks.
A) talking · B) talk · C) to talk · D) talked
→ **A) talking** — *Piège :* **avoid + -ing**.

**51.** The firm succeeded ___ raising €50m.
A) for · B) on · C) to · D) in
→ **D) in** — *Piège :* **succeed in + -ing**.

**52.** He is good ___ negotiations.
A) in · B) on · C) for · D) at
→ **D) at** — *Piège :* **good at** (compétence).

**53.** These results differ ___ last year's.
A) with · B) from · C) to · D) of
→ **B) from** — *Piège :* **differ from**.

**54.** This product is superior ___ the old one.
A) of · B) from · C) than · D) to
→ **D) to** — *Piège :* **superior to** (pas « than », malgré le comparatif).

**55.** We spent €2m ___ marketing.
A) in · B) at · C) for · D) on
→ **D) on** — *Piège :* **spend money on**.

**56.** She is keen ___ expanding abroad.
A) at · B) to · C) in · D) on
→ **D) on** — *Piège :* **keen on + -ing**.

**57.** The audit complied ___ the new standards.
A) on · B) to · C) for · D) with
→ **D) with** — *Piège :* **comply with**.

**58.** The contract is subject ___ board approval.
A) to · B) with · C) for · D) of
→ **A) to** — *Piège :* **subject to**.

**59.** Please refer ___ the appendix for details.
A) to · B) on · C) for · D) at
→ **A) to** — *Piège :* **refer to**.

**60.** The manager insisted ___ an internal review.
A) in · B) for · C) on · D) at
→ **C) on** — *Piège :* **insist on**.

## B. Vocabulaire — quel mot correspond à la définition ?

*Piège général : les leurres ressemblent au mot cible (defer/infer/confer) ou en sont le contraire. Lis la définition en entier avant de choisir.*

**1.** to postpone or delay an event
A) infer · B) defer · C) confer · D) refer
→ **B) defer** — *Piège :* leurres de forme : infer=déduire, refer=renvoyer, confer=consulter.

**2.** to deduce from evidence
A) confer · B) refer · C) infer · D) defer
→ **C) infer** — *Piège :* infer = déduire ; ne pas confondre avec defer (reporter).

**3.** to consult someone before deciding
A) infer · B) prefer · C) confer · D) defer
→ **C) confer** — *Piège :* confer = se concerter.

**4.** money lent that must be repaid
A) debt · B) loan · C) lease · D) deposit
→ **B) loan** — *Piège :* loan = le prêt accordé ; debt = la dette due.

**5.** money that is owed
A) credit · B) asset · C) loan · D) debt
→ **D) debt** — *Piège :* debt = somme due (le « b » est muet).

**6.** the person who receives payment
A) debtor · B) payer · C) payee · D) broker
→ **C) payee** — *Piège :* payee = bénéficiaire ; payer = payeur.

**7.** an official order to pay money
A) warrant · B) warranty · C) warning · D) warden
→ **A) warrant** — *Piège :* warrant = mandat/ordre ; warranty = garantie produit.

**8.** a written promise to repair or replace
A) warrant · B) warden · C) warranty · D) warehouse
→ **C) warranty** — *Piège :* warranty = garantie commerciale.

**9.** the amount by which revenue exceeds cost
A) margin · B) turnover · C) revenue · D) profit
→ **D) profit** — *Piège :* profit = résultat net ; turnover = chiffre d'affaires.

**10.** the total income from sales
A) yield · B) revenue · C) dividend · D) profit
→ **B) revenue** — *Piège :* revenue/turnover = CA, avant charges.

**11.** a payment made to shareholders
A) interest · B) dividend · C) royalty · D) coupon
→ **B) dividend** — *Piège :* dividend = dividende ; interest = intérêt (dette).

**12.** the income earned on a loan or deposit
A) interest · B) dividend · C) yield · D) fee
→ **A) interest** — *Piège :* interest = intérêt (prêt/dépôt).

**13.** to officially cancel a law or contract
A) recall · B) recover · C) repeal · D) revoke
→ **D) revoke** — *Piège :* revoke = révoquer ; repeal = abroger une loi.

**14.** to say again or restate
A) iterate · B) reiterate · C) irrigate · D) integrate
→ **B) reiterate** — *Piège :* reiterate = réaffirmer.

**15.** to examine official records
A) assess · B) account · C) audit · D) arbitrate
→ **C) audit** — *Piège :* audit = vérification formelle des comptes.

**16.** a person who buys shares
A) auditor · B) creditor · C) investor · D) debtor
→ **C) investor** — *Piège :* investor = investisseur ; creditor = créancier.

**17.** a person or firm owed money
A) investor · B) creditor · C) debtor · D) borrower
→ **B) creditor** — *Piège :* creditor = créancier ; debtor = débiteur.

**18.** a person who owes money
A) payee · B) lender · C) debtor · D) creditor
→ **C) debtor** — *Piège :* debtor = débiteur (celui qui doit).

**19.** to give up a right or claim
A) weave · B) wave · C) waver · D) waive
→ **D) waive** — *Piège :* waive = renoncer à ; wave = saluer/vague (piège de sens).

**20.** a rise or growth in value or size
A) increase · B) interest · C) increment · D) decrease
→ **A) increase** — *Piège :* increase (nom) = augmentation ; increment = pas d'incrémentation.

**21.** a fall in value or size
A) increase · B) deduction · C) decrease · D) deficit
→ **C) decrease** — *Piège :* decrease = baisse.

**22.** money kept back from a payment

→ **D) deduction** — *Piège :* deduction = retenue ; discount = remise.

**23.** a reduction in price granted to a buyer

**24.** a partial refund after purchase

**25.** money paid regularly for the use of property

**26.** a contract giving the right to use property

**35.** a person who arranges deals for others

**36.** a person who buys and sells for their own account

**37.** a person legally managing assets for another

**38.** to state the value of an asset
A) apprise · B) appraise · C) praise · D) raise

**39.** to inform someone

→ **A) apprise** — *Piège :* apprise = informer.

**40.** to make an asset less valuable on the books
A) depreciate · B) deflate · C) dilute · D) devalue
→ **A) depreciate** — *Piège :* depreciate = amortir (comptable) ; devalue = dévaluer (monnaie).

**41.** to spread the cost of an asset over its life

**43.** to set aside money for a specific purpose

**44.** the total value of a company's shares

→ **B) market capitalisation** — *Piège :* market cap = cours × nombre d'actions.

**45.** the value of assets minus liabilities
A) net assets · B) turnover · C) market cap · D) gross assets

**46.** profit expressed as a percentage of revenue

**47.** income from an investment, in %

**48.** money borrowed by a company via bonds
