> *Why do French SMEs choose a bank vs. a fintech for FX hedging?*
> This thesis investigates the determinants of FX dealer choice by French SMEs for forwards/swaps (EUR/USD, EUR/MAD). While pricing is observable (bid-ask spreads, forward points), corporates often stay with their bank despite cheaper fintech quotes (Ebury, iBanFirst). Using a mixed-methods design, we collect anonymized price quotes from 3 banks and 2 fintechs for identical trades, and conduct 12-15 semi-structured interviews with treasurers and FX sales. We test whether price, platform speed, advisory quality and trust/relationship (including ESG perception) explain dealer choice. The project leverages our complementary backgrounds (Corporate Financial Management + Financial Institution Front Office/Trade Finance) and provides actionable insights for corporate FX sales.
