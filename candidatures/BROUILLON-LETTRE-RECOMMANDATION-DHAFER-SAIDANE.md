As Professor of Finance and Director of the MSc Corporate Financial Management at SKEMA Business School, I am pleased to recommend Mr. Calvin Minang.

I taught Calvin in the course Money, Banking and Financial Markets and interacted regularly with him through his role as elected Class Representative of the MSc CFM cohort.

Throughout the semester, Calvin demonstrated a strong interest in banking, financial markets and foreign exchange markets. He actively contributed to classroom discussions on topics including banking regulation, financial risks, sustainable finance, foreign exchange markets and the role of financial institutions in the economy. His participation reflected curiosity, critical thinking and a genuine interest in financial market dynamics.

In both group projects and class presentations, Calvin displayed solid analytical and communication skills, with the ability to synthesize and explain complex financial concepts effectively.

As Class Representative, he carried out his responsibilities with professionalism, reliability and commitment, serving as a constructive liaison between students and faculty.

Our discussions also highlighted his particular interest in foreign exchange markets and international finance, which was further enriched by his international academic semester in Brazil and which he intends to explore in depth through his Master's thesis.

Based on my observations, I am confident that Calvin possesses the analytical, interpersonal and professional qualities required to succeed in demanding academic and professional environments, particularly within financial markets, sales & trading and Front Office functions.

I recommend him with confidence.

Yours sincerely,

Prof. Dhafer Saidane
Professor of Finance
Director, MSc Corporate Financial Management
